NEXA Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NEXA ("the bot", "we", "our") collects, uses, and protects information while providing services through Discord.

1. Information We Collect

NEXA does not collect or store personal information permanently. Limited technical data may be processed temporarily in order to execute bot commands or features, including:

  • User and server IDs (used to identify the Discord environment)
  • Message content directly related to a command
  • Voice connection or playback data for music features

All such data is automatically cleared once the command or session ends.

2. Payments and Purchases

When users make purchases or subscriptions related to NEXA, all payment processing is handled by Discord or its authorized third-party payment providers. NEXA itself does not collect, store, or have access to any credit card, billing, or financial information. Any billing disputes or refund requests should be directed to the official payment platform used during purchase.

3. Data Sharing

NEXA does not share any collected data with third parties. Data may be accessed only by the bot owner for debugging or performance monitoring, and never for commercial use.

4. Data Security

All connections between NEXA and Discord are secured using HTTPS and the official Discord API. No sensitive data is ever stored outside the Discord platform or its authorized services.

5. User Rights

You may request deletion of any stored information or inquire about how data is handled by contacting us directly via the email address below.

6. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any significant changes will be published on this page with a revision date.
