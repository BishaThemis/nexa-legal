NEXA Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how NEXA ("the bot", "we", "our") collects, uses, and protects information while providing AI-driven community management services through Discord and connected platforms like Kick.

1. Information We Collect & Store

To provide personalized AI interactions, NEXA collects and stores specific data. This includes:

  • User and server IDs (to identify users across Discord).
  • Connected platform IDs (e.g., Kick usernames if linked by the user or server).
  • Interaction History & "Memory": NEXA analyzes chat context to build a dynamic "Emotion Memory" and "Personality Score" to improve AI interactions.
  • Voice Data: For features like Voice Transcription (STT), audio is processed momentarily but never recorded or stored permanently.

2. Third-Party AI Services

NEXA is powered by advanced Large Language Models (LLMs). To generate responses, analyze sentiment, or process audio, necessary text or voice snippets are securely transmitted to third-party AI providers (such as Google Gemini, Groq, or voice API providers). These providers process the data solely to return the requested output and do not use it to train public models.

3. Payments and Purchases

When users make purchases or subscriptions related to NEXA, all payment processing is handled by authorized third-party providers. NEXA does not collect or store financial information.

4. Data Security & Sharing

We do not sell or share your profile or memory data with advertisers. Data is strictly used to maintain the bot's functionality and personality within your server.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to be forgotten. You can request the deletion of your specific "Memory" and interaction data from NEXA's database at any time by using the appropriate bot command or contacting us.
